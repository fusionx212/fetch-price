How it works

Fetch Price is an early asset detection aggregator for small trade buyers. We pull together timing signals and market prices so you see what kit might be worth and what to offer — before you waste a day at the wrong yard.

We are not an auction house. We do not sell you raw listings to dig through. We give you a decision: is this trip worth it, and what’s your max bid?

Why dealers use us

  • Stop overbidding — know the retail ceiling before you get emotional at the gate.
  • Stop missing margin — see the spread between auction clearance and retail on similar kit.
  • Stop arriving late — pair with RCD weekly packs so the number lands with Monday’s distress matches.

Example: independent van dealer

Situation: You run a six-bay yard outside Birmingham. A rumour goes round that a Coventry haulier is in trouble — three Transits on the yard.

Without Fetch Price: You drive over, get swept up in the room, bid £24k because you’re scared to lose the lot, then discover similar vans retail at £27k but auction lots in the patch have been clearing at £18–19k. Thin deal — or a loss after prep.

With Fetch Price + RCD weekly packs: Monday’s pack flags the distress match. Fetch Price shows a matched spread on 2021 Transits: ~£18,500 at auction vs ~£27,500 retail — about £9k room to work. You ring the liquidator at £19,500 knowing your walk-away. You either win at a fair margin or you don’t go at all.

Result: One decision made at the kitchen table, not in a crowded yard with your heart rate up.

What you actually receive

  • A clearance band (low / mid / high) for the asset class — vans, plant, workshop kit, fleet units.
  • A retail reference on similar listings so you see the gap.
  • The spread — that’s the product. Everything else is noise.

Delivered in the weekly pack when a match is in that run — not as a wall of unrelated search results.
